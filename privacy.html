Privacy Policy

App: Bible Study App (com.waitaminutedigital.biblestudy)

Developer: Waitaminute Digital

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Waitaminute Digital built the Bible Study App as an offline-first scripture reading application. This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data.

2. User Accounts and Personal Data

Bible Study App does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, request, or store personal identifiers such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.

3. Offline Storage

All scripture reading progress, custom study notes, search history, and verse highlights remain stored locally on your device storage. None of this data is transmitted, monitored, or sold.

4. Diagnostic and Analytics Data

The app may transmit anonymized diagnostic information and crash reports via Google Firebase Analytics to improve application performance and stability. This technical data includes:

  • Crash logs and system diagnostic metrics
  • Anonymous installation identifiers and device hardware models

This data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS and is not linked to any personal user identity.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with Google Play Services and Firebase for telemetry and store delivery. For more details, consult the Google Privacy Policy.
